# COMP6012 Week 12 — Security UX Lab

Interactive Week 12 lecture site for **Usable Security: Threat Intelligence as Part of the Process**.

## Concept

A bright **Security UX Lab** rather than a slide deck. The central question is:

> Can we make the secure choice the easy choice?

The site follows the lecture from threat modeling and least privilege through intelligence-led testing, third-party risk, privacy, social engineering, mental models and intelligence-based DevSecOps architecture.

## Actual classroom games

1. **Permission Sculptor** — give bank tellers, doctors and developers enough authority to work without over-privileging them.
2. **Build the Pen Test** — sequence the six intelligence-led penetration-testing stages.
3. **Risk Triage** — combine threat, vulnerability and asset for realistic scenarios.
4. **Security or Privacy?** — classify cases where protection and lawful/appropriate data use differ.
5. **Phishing Inbox** — inspect realistic simulated messages and identify which deserve escalation.
6. **Fix the Security UI** — choose designs that correctly use affordances, constraints and conventions.
7. **Knowledge Flow** — explore Design, Build, Run and Defend and match intelligence to the right phase.
8. **Final Challenge** — ten questions with immediate feedback and accumulated Lab XP.

## Lecture coverage

- DevOps → DevSecOps and shifting security left
- Least privilege, APT29/UAC bypass and AppleJeus consent design
- Intelligence-led penetration testing and APT12 profiling
- Source-code analysis and third-party risk
- Threat + vulnerability + asset
- Equifax patch-management lesson
- Data protection vs privacy, PII, biometrics and regulation
- Social engineering, campaign examples and the five-step phishing process
- Assessment campaigns and mental models
- Affordances, constraints and conventions
- Defendable architecture
- Design / Build / Run / Defend knowledge flow
- Enterprise security layers
- Visibility, manageability and survivability

Every major module includes three **flip-in-place deep-dive cards** with additional PPT detail.
